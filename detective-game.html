Detective game

Play detective

Two ways to play with the same NASA series. Case files hand you one variable that changed and a list of suspects; story mode walks a younger detective through one place with a year dial and three questions.

How scoring works

Strong clue · 100 pts

|r| ≥ 0.5 at some lag from −6 to +6 months, and you picked one of the strongest suspects.

Weak clue · 40 pts

|r| between 0.3 and 0.5 — a real link, but it explains less of the story.

Red herring · 0 pts

|r| below 0.3 — the series simply do not move together, whatever the story suggests.

Every r is computed in your browser from the NASA series for that region, using de-seasonalised monthly values. Nothing is scripted, so a different time range would give a different answer.

A clear path to evidence

How it works

Four steps, about a minute of clicking, and one honest answer: is this really changing — and if it is, what does that change do?

  1. 01

    Pick a place on the map

    Choose one of six regions, one of eight NASA variables, a time range and a resolution.

  2. 02

    See the trend

    Three decades of observations become one chart, with the trend line and its uncertainty.

  3. 03

    Get the verdict

    A stamp tells you if the change is significant, and a plain sentence tells you what it means.

  4. 04

    Trace cause and effect

    Compare it with a second place, then trace what the change is associated with — and what comes after it.

Why we detect at all

A trend is a clue, not the answer.

Detecting the change is only the first half. The reason to detect anything is the application behind it: knowing what causes this change, and what this change affects. That is what the other two rooms are for — Compare tests whether two places under the same large-scale process move together, and the Detective game follows the chain from soil to rain to the people who depend on it.
